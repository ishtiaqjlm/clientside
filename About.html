About Ethiopia

Explore the history, geography, and people of this ancient land.

History

Ethiopia is one of the world’s oldest nations, home to ancient human origins and early civilizations like Dʿmt and Aksum. The Aksumite Empire became a major power and adopted Christianity in the 4th century. Medieval Ethiopia, led by the Solomonic dynasty, remained independent and defeated Italy at the Battle of Adwa in 1896.

Geography

Ethiopia is a mountainous country with highlands, plateaus, and many rivers—including the Blue Nile, which contributes about 86% of the Nile.

People & Languages

Ethiopia is a diverse country with over 120 ethnic groups and more than 80 languages, including Amharic, Oromo, Tigrinya, and Somali.
